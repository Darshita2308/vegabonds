About the band

Raw stories, neon nights, restless rhythm.

VAGABONDS is a Udaipur-based indie collective blending cinematic rock, underground electronic textures, and poetic street narratives. Their live set feels like a late-night film score—raw, atmospheric, and unforgettable.

Cinematic Rock

Bold riffs and ambient layers that feel like a city after midnight.

Street Poetry

Lyric-driven storytelling with a restless, modern edge.

Live Magnetism

Every show is designed to hit like a neon-lit memory.
